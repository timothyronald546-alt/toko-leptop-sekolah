Our Journey

The Story Behind STRIK LEPTOP

From a vocational student to an inspiring laptop entrepreneur.

About Our Store

My name is Raditia, a vocational student who discovered a passion for laptops while working part-time. Inspired to turn my skills into a business, I partnered with my friend Sebastian to create STRIK LEPTOP.

Even after being unexpectedly let go from my job, I saw it as an opportunity rather than a setback. We started small, focusing on added value—carefully inspecting devices, upgrading hardware, and guiding customers.

From every transaction, I learned that business is about trust, service, and courage. Today, what began as a late-night idea continues to grow, driven by resilience and a commitment to learning.
